What if MAD is
a failure of intelligence?

MAP emerged from trying to sublimate deterrence logic: keep the stabilizing insight that relationships matter, but change what the relationship makes mutually durable.

MAD stabilizes through vulnerability.I cannot defect because you can destroy me. The relation preserves an equilibrium by preserving reciprocal threat.
