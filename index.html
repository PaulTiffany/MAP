Mutually Assured Progress

Progress
that transfers.

What if the stable outcome of interaction were not reciprocal threat, but reciprocal capacity to improve?

MAP is a research program about the conditions under which progress propagates between adaptive systems — and the conditions under which it does not.
01 / The inversion

From deterrence
to transfer.

MAD makes defection expensive. MAP asks whether we can make continued mutual progress structurally easier to preserve.

Mutually Assured Destruction

MAD

“I cannot defect because you can destroy me.”

Equilibrium through reciprocal vulnerability.

Mutually Assured Progress

MAP

“Your progress can increase the durability of mine.”

Candidate equilibrium through reciprocal capacity.
