# Dzanga — living rainforest demo

A flagship demonstration of the semantic-motion engine using the rainforests of the northwestern Congo Basin as visual and ecological reference.

The demo is **not** a simulation of BaAka life and does not reproduce ritual, sacred imagery, dress, or recorded music. The nod is deeper: treat the forest as a many-voiced, relational system whose paths, clearings, animals, water, plants, and human knowledge coexist without collapsing into one sovereign viewpoint.

Reference landscape: the Sangha Trinational / Dzanga-Sangha region of southwestern Central African Republic.

## Purpose

Prove that the GitHub-Pages-friendly engine can combine:

- free pan and pointer-centered semantic zoom;
- scroll-scrubbed guided camera choreography;
- visual effects driven by story progress, zoom depth, and scroll activity at once;
- stable semantic object IDs independent of art assets;
- a rich SVG world that remains inspectable and replaceable.

## Files

- `DEMO_VISION.md` — experience and success criteria.
- `CULTURAL_GROUNDING.md` — respectful-use constraints and research notes.
- `FOREST_STYLE.md` — cartographic and motion language.
- `VERTICAL_SLICE.md` — the first complete route: river → closed canopy → bai.
- `PERF_BUDGET.md` — browser/GitHub Pages constraints.
- `world.json` — semantic object/LOD contract.
- `motion.json` — authored choreography.
- `index.html` — executable first slice.

The platform rule remains: **scroll is the clock; zoom is semantic resolution; position is context; velocity is expressive energy.**
