# Reference sources

These sources inform geography, ecology, and the project's restrained BaAka acknowledgement. They are references, not licenses to reproduce photography, recordings, stories, or cultural material.

## Landscape / ecology

- UNESCO World Heritage Centre — **Sangha Trinational**  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1380

- Dzanga-Sangha Protected Areas — **Natural heritage and biodiversity**  
  https://dzanga-sangha.org/facts-infos/natural-heritage-and-biodiversity/

- Dzanga-Sangha Protected Areas — **Bais: forest elephant paradise in the middle of the rainforest**  
  https://dzanga-sangha.org/stories/bais/

- Dzanga-Sangha Protected Areas — **Facts and Infos**  
  https://dzanga-sangha.org/facts-infos/

## BaAka grounding

- Dzanga-Sangha Protected Areas — **Indigenous Peoples / Cultural heritage**  
  https://dzanga-sangha.org/facts-infos/cultural-heritage/

- Michelle Kisliuk — **Seize the Dance! Baaka Musical Life and the Ethnography of Performance**, Oxford University Press.  
  https://academic.oup.com/book/49277

- World History Commons — **Analyzing Music**, Michelle Kisliuk; overview of BaAka polyphonic/polyrhythmic performance and structured improvisation.  
  https://worldhistorycommons.org/analyzing-music

## Use rule

Facts may guide original graphics and interaction architecture. Do not copy photographs, audio, songs, ritual imagery, or ethnographic narratives into the demo without separate rights/provenance review.
