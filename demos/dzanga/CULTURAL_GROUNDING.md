# Cultural grounding

## Scope of the nod

The demo is inspired by the rainforests of the Sangha Trinational / Dzanga-Sangha landscape and offered as a respectful nod to BaAka people whose knowledge and daily life are deeply connected with that forest.

The project should not imply that this fictional interface is an authentic BaAka map, cosmology, ritual system, or artwork.

## What we may borrow

Use public ecological and ethnographic facts as design principles:

- the forest is a connected mosaic rather than a single undifferentiated green mass;
- bais are natural openings that make hidden ecological relations visible;
- rivers, swamp forest, closed canopy, animal paths, and seasonal resources create overlapping movement systems;
- BaAka knowledge of the forest is substantial and materially important to research, conservation, and local life;
- BaAka musical practice is well documented as polyphonic and polyrhythmic, with structured improvisation among multiple participants.

The last point may inspire **orchestration architecture**: independent visual voices can enter, answer, overlap, and remain legible without being collapsed into a single master animation. Do not reproduce melodies, ritual structures, recordings, or ceremonial names unless a later project has explicit provenance and permission.

## What we should not do

- no invented 'tribal' glyphs;
- no generic masks, body paint, huts, weapons, or costume as decorative motifs;
- no imitation of sacred/ritual practices;
- no sampled BaAka recordings in the demo;
- no claim that our fantasy geography represents BaAka cosmology;
- no use of the historically loaded blanket term `pygmy` in project prose except when accurately quoting or discussing a source;
- no treating Indigenous people as timeless or outside modern economic and political life.

## Representation rule

For v1, **do not depict BaAka people at all**. Let the acknowledgement live in the project's framing and in the orchestration principle. The rendered world should focus on forest systems.

If later versions portray people, language, music, stories, or named cultural practices, that becomes a separate provenance/consultation task.

## Reference facts used for art direction

- Dzanga-Sangha sits in the northwestern Congo Basin and contains tropical forest, wetland, streams, marshes, and natural clearings.
- Forest clearings known as bais can concentrate mineral-rich water/soil and attract forest elephants, gorillas, antelope, buffalo, and other wildlife.
- Forest elephants disperse seeds over long distances and materially reshape the forest.
- BaAka communities live in and around Dzanga-Sangha, and local sources describe their forest knowledge as important to science, conservation, tourism, and cultural continuity.
- Ethnomusicological literature describes BaAka performance as polyphonic/polyrhythmic and socially interactive. We use that only as an abstract coordination metaphor.

## Design maxim

**Reference relationship, not stereotype.**

The respectful nod should be visible in how the system thinks — many agents, many layers, no single sovereign view — more than in surface decoration.
