# Living atlas interaction model

The MAP site is one continuous semantic geography.

- Free roam: pan and zoom anywhere in the atlas.
- Semantic zoom: geography resolves into relations, mechanisms, and formal objects as scale increases.
- Guided trails: entering Origin, Theory, Application, or Intuition turns scroll into an authored scrubber through that region while remaining inside the same world.
- Scroll-triggered graphics are stateful parts of the geography: bridges grow, threat fades, transfer markers move, viability regions expand, application networks assemble, and the Lean kernel draws as the reader advances.
- Exiting a trail returns to free-roam without changing the underlying map.

The design goal is not a page decorated like a map. The map is the interface and the explanation.
