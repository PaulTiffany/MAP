# MAP world

The atlas uses a fantasy-cartography metaphor to make semantic structure spatial.

- The center is MAP itself.
- Origin is a western deterrence landscape.
- Theory is a northern highland of membranes, transfer, viability, and convergence.
- Applications form an eastern networked territory.
- Intuition is a southern valley where local improvement becomes relational progress.

These are not separate pages. They are regions in one world that share coordinates, roads, depth, and visual language. Zoom changes semantic resolution; scroll along a guided trail changes explanatory state.
